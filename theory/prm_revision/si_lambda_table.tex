species and Yukawa screening lengths $\lambda^*$, ordered by atomic
number.  All values are in \AA.  $\lambda^*$ is computed from the
branch-continuous inversion
$\lambda^*=(-R_0^*+\sqrt{R_0^{*2}+4B^*R_0^*})/2$ in the main text,
which is real for $B^*\ge -R_0^*/4$.  For the 101 screening-branch
species ($B^*>0$) it is positive and physical.  For the two
anti-screening species (Mo$^{3+}$ and Sb$^{3+}$, $B^*<0$) the same
root is negative and is tabulated as a signed Yukawa-branch
diagnostic rather than a physical screening length, because the
first-order expansion does not adequately describe their bonding.
The second root of the same quadratic,
$\lambda^*_-=(-R_0^*-\sqrt{R_0^{*2}+4B^*R_0^*})/2$, is listed for
every species in the $\lambda^*_-$ column.  On the anti-screening
branch it also lies in the admissible interval $(-R_0^*,0)$.  On
the screening branch it lies below $-R_0^*$, outside the
admissible interval, and is reported for completeness.
$n_{\mathrm{CN}}$ is the number of distinct coordination shells used
to determine $(R_0^*,B^*)$.
Parenthetical uncertainties on $B^*$ are the weighted standard deviation
$\sigma_B$ of the $n$-resolved lines at $R_0^*$.  Uncertainties on
$\lambda^*$ are propagated via
$\sigma_\lambda=|\partial\lambda^*/\partial B^*|\,\sigma_B$.
Entries with $n_{\mathrm{CN}}=2$ have $\sigma_B=0$ by construction
and should be regarded as provisional.}
